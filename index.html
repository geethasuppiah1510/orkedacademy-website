YOUR TEAM. YOUR PRIORITIES.

The right fit.
Before we begin.

Choose the skills that matter to your people. Work with Geetha to shape the session around them.

Which programme fits our team?+

Choose professional presence when your priority is grooming, attire and the impression your people make. Choose etiquette when the focus is introductions, conversations and professional occasions. If both matter, select “Both programmes” in your enquiry.

Can the learning reflect our workplace?+

Yes. Share your team’s roles, the standards you want to strengthen and the situations they encounter. Those details help Geetha make the learning relevant to your organisation.

How long is a programme?+

Scope and duration are agreed with Geetha around your objectives and group. Include your preferred timing and team size so you can discuss a suitable format.

What should we include in an enquiry?+

Tell us who the training is for, roughly how many people will attend and what you want them to handle more confidently. A preferred date and location are useful if you have them.

Can we discuss fees and HRD Corp claims?+

Ask Geetha for a proposal based on your scope and group. If you intend to claim through HRD Corp, confirm the specific programme’s eligibility and requirements with her before booking.
